How ReliefChain AI Works

A transparent, closed-loop pipeline from emergency request to verified physical handover.

1
📍

Citizen SOS Intake

Affected citizens report disaster type, GPS location, affected population count, and urgent resource requirements.

2
🧠

AI Triage Scoring

The Decision Support System evaluates disaster severity, medical trauma, hazard scores, and wait times to assign transparent 0-100 priority tiers.

3
🏭

NGO Depot Dispatch

Relief organizations allocate supplies from warehouse stock with automatic over-allocation prevention and inventory reservation.

4
🔐

QR Ledger Verification

Volunteers scan single-use cryptographic QR tokens upon handover. GPS coordinates and tamper-evident SHA-256 block hashes seal the delivery.
