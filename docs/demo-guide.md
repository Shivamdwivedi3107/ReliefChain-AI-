# ReliefChain AI — Presentation & Demonstration Guide

This guide provides a structured 5-to-10 minute presentation script and walkthrough for hackathon judges, university professors, and project evaluators.

---

## ⏱️ Recommended Presentation Flow (7-Minute Pitch)

| Timestamp | Section | Key Action on UI | Talking Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0:00 - 1:00** | **The Crisis & Problem** | Overview Tab (`/ui/`) | *"When disasters strike, emergency lines freeze and relief aid becomes a logistical black hole. ReliefChain AI unifies AI triage, SPHERE supply forecasting, and cryptographic proof-of-delivery."* |
| **1:00 - 2:00** | **Disaster Story Mode** | Story Mode (`/ui/` -> Story Mode) | Click `▶️ Auto Play`. Show the 9-step animated narrative demonstrating satellite alert ingestion to blockchain handover. |
| **2:00 - 3:00** | **Citizen Experience** | Click Persona: `Citizen` -> One-Tap SOS | Click `🚨 One-Tap Emergency SOS`. Move people slider to `6`, check `Medical`, show instant AI Priority 1 (Critical) calculation preview, and click Dispatch. |
| **3:00 - 4:00** | **AI Copilot & Incident Command** | Click Persona: `Admin` -> AI Copilot | Click prompt chip: `🚨 Show Critical Incidents` and `📦 Find Resource Shortages`. Show how the Copilot returns telemetry-verified actionable directives. |
| **4:00 - 5:00** | **Digital Twin & Shortage Radar** | Digital Twin & Shortage Radar tabs | Move severity slider to `9.0` to watch SPHERE water and ration needs scale live. Switch to Shortage Radar to see color-coded `RED` stockouts. |
| **5:00 - 6:00** | **Volunteer Smart Ops & QR Verification** | Click Persona: `Volunteer` | Show the `94% AI Match` mission recommendation. Demonstrate how single-use QR verification burns the token and captures GPS. |
| **6:00 - 7:00** | **Cryptographic Transparency Journey** | Transparency Journey Tab | Search or click a verified journey to show the 6-stage pipeline with SHA-256 Merkle-linked hashes. Conclude with the Pitch Deck comparison matrix. |

---

## 🎭 Persona Switching Shortcuts

Use the top **Persona Switcher** bar to jump between user contexts instantly:
- **Admin**: Shows full Incident Command Center, active threat gauges, and DevOps telemetry.
- **Field Volunteer**: Shows assigned missions, workload capacity gauges, and AI match scores.
- **Citizen Resident**: Shows safe shelters, active SOS distress status, and One-Tap SOS.
- **Relief NGO**: Shows inventory depot balances and supply reservation locks.
- **Aid Donor**: Shows the public verifiable transparency journey and ledger blocks.
