# ReliefChain AI — Project Presentation & College Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Project**: ReliefChain AI
- **Tagline**: Intelligent Humanitarian Aid Coordination, AI Emergency Triage & Cryptographically Verifiable Transparency Ledger
- **Presenter**: Senior Full-Stack AI & Systems Engineer
- **Core Pitch**: Transforming chaotic disaster logistics into a deterministic, AI-guided, and 100% auditable humanitarian aid pipeline.

---

## Slide 2: The Humanitarian Crisis Problem
- **Problem 1 (Triage Bottleneck)**: During cyclones and floods, emergency dispatchers receive 10,000+ unverified calls per hour. Critical trauma cases are delayed behind non-urgent requests.
- **Problem 2 (Supply Chain Black Hole)**: Donors and NGOs have zero real-time visibility into warehouse stockouts or whether aid packages actually reached beneficiaries.
- **Problem 3 (Ghost Deliveries & Fraud)**: Paper receipts enable diversion and double-dipping without verifiable proof-of-delivery.

---

## Slide 3: The ReliefChain AI Solution
1. **Explainable AI Triage**: Dual-layer Random Forest + rule safety clamps scores distress urgency in `<10ms`.
2. **Dynamic SPHERE Shortage Radar**: Evaluates depot warehouse stock against international humanitarian benchmarks in real time.
3. **Smart Volunteer Dispatch**: 4-factor multi-criteria ranking matches the closest, most qualified, and available first responders.
4. **Single-Use Cryptographic QR Verification**: GPS-verified handover with instant nonce burning and Merkle-linked SHA-256 block ledger sealing.
